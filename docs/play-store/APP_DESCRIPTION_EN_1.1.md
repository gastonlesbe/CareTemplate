# PetPal App Description - English (Updated v1.1)

## 📄 Full Description (Updated with new features)

```
🐾 PetPal - Your pet care assistant

PetPal is the perfect app to keep everything related to your pets' care organized. Never forget a vaccine, a vet visit, or an important treatment again.

✨ MAIN FEATURES:

📅 Smart Reminders

Create personalized events for each pet: vaccines, vet visits, deworming, grooming, and more. Receive notifications so you never forget anything important.

💰 Expense Tracking

Record all expenses related to your pets: vet, food, toys, accessories. Keep complete control of your finances.

🐕 Multiple Pets

Manage all your pets in one place. Each one with its own profile, events, and expenses.

☁️ Cloud Synchronization

Your data automatically syncs with Firebase, so you can access it from any device. Improved and more reliable synchronization in version 1.1.

🔄 Share Pets (NEW in v1.1)

Now you can share your pets' information with other users using a unique code. Perfect for sharing with veterinarians, family members, or caregivers. Ideal when you need someone else to have access to your pet's information.

🎨 Intuitive Interface

Modern and easy-to-use design. Navigate quickly between your pets and events. Floating button available on all screens to quickly and easily add events or pets.

🔔 Notifications

Receive automatic reminders of important events so you never forget to care for your furry companions.

📊 Complete Organization

Keep a complete history of all your pets' events and expenses. Perfect for maintaining a detailed record of their health and well-being.

---

Why choose PetPal?

• Simple and easy to use

• Designed specifically for pets

• Improved automatic synchronization

• Share information with veterinarians and caregivers

• No complications, just functionality

• Improved and more intuitive interface

Start taking better care of your pets today. Download PetPal and give your furry companions the care they deserve.

🐾 Because they're also part of the family!
```
